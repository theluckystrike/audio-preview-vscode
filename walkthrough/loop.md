# Loop a region

- Drag across the waveform or the spectrogram to mark a region. Looping turns on.
- Press `[` and `]` to set the start and end at the playhead.
- Press `L` to turn the loop on or off. Double-click the waveform to clear it.
- Pick a speed from 0.25x to 2x. The pitch stays the same.
- `Space` plays and pauses. The arrow keys jump 5 seconds (1 second with Shift).
