# Read the loudness

The Loudness panel follows ITU-R BS.1770-4 (the method behind EBU R128 and streaming loudness targets).

| Value | What it means |
|---|---|
| Integrated | Gated loudness of the whole file, in LUFS |
| Short-term max | Loudest 3 second window |
| Momentary max | Loudest 400 ms window |
| Sample peak | Highest sample, in dBFS (not true peak) |
| RMS | Unweighted RMS of all channels |
| Clipped samples | Samples at full scale |

Click **Copy stats** to put the numbers on the clipboard as plain text.
